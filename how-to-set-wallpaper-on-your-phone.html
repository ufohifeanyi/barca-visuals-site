How to Set Any Wallpaper on Your Phone (Android & iPhone)

9 October 2026 · Barça Visuals

Got a new wallpaper from our gallery? Setting it takes less than a minute.

On Android

  • Download the image from the wallpaper page. It saves to your Gallery or Photos app.
  • Open the image, tap the three dots (⋮), then choose Set as wallpaper.
  • Pick Home screen, Lock screen or Both, then confirm.

On iPhone

  • Download the image, then open it in Photos.
  • Tap the Share icon and choose Use as Wallpaper.
  • Pinch to adjust the position, then tap Add.

Tips for a sharp result

  • Choose phone-sized (tall) wallpapers for your phone and wide ones for desktop.
  • Turn off "Perspective Zoom" on iPhone if the image looks cropped.
