Jules Koundé's Barça Story in Numbers

12 October 2026 · Barça Visuals

Few players in this Barça squad are as hard to pin to one position as Jules Koundé. The club lists him as a centre-back who can also play at full-back, and in Barcelona it is mostly on the right side that he has made his name.

Quick facts

  • Born: 12 November 1998, Paris. French and Beninese heritage.
  • Height: 181 cm. Shirt number: 23.
  • Clubs: Bordeaux (2017–19), Sevilla (2019–22), Barcelona (since 2022).
  • Barça debut: 28 August 2022.
  • Barça appearances: 194, the most by any French player in the club's history. He passed Éric Abidal (193) and Ousmane Dembélé (185), according to French Football Weekly on 10 October 2026.

The path to Barcelona

Koundé began his senior career at Bordeaux, then moved to Sevilla in 2019 and won the Europa League there in 2019–20. Barcelona agreed his signing in July 2022, and he made his debut the following month.

Moments that define his Barça years

  • Copa del Rey 2025: he scored the extra-time winner against Real Madrid as Barça won the final 3–2.
  • Supercopa 2025: he provided an assist in the 5–2 final.
  • Frankfurt, 9 December 2025: he scored twice against Eintracht Frankfurt in the Champions League.
  • Silverware: La Liga in 2022–23, 2024–25 and 2025–26, the Copa del Rey in 2024–25, and the Supercopa in 2023, 2025 and 2026.

This season

Last season (2025–26) he played 47 games and scored 3 goals. This season the picture is different. French Football Weekly reported that the game against Getafe, after the October international break, was only his third start of the campaign, with Eric García competing for the right-back spot and Hansi Flick often favouring García.

For France he has 56 caps (as of July 2026), was a World Cup finalist in 2022 and was named in the squad for the 2026 World Cup.

Our take

This part is opinion. Koundé's value has never been about highlight reels. It is about covering space, defending one-against-one and being trusted in finals. When a player can fill two positions at that level, a coach rarely lets him leave the squad, and a quieter start to a season is not the same as a drop in quality. We will watch how his minutes develop over the next few league games.

Transfer talk (rumour, not confirmed)

Some outlets, including Barca Blaugranes on 10 October 2026, have run headlines about possible January interest from Bayern Munich and Chelsea. No club has confirmed anything, so treat it as speculation. We will update this page if that changes.
